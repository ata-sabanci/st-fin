01
Path one

TradingView AI Chart Copilot

The Copilot reads your chart in real time. Activate one lens, ask it to analyze, and explore interactively — no exporting, no switching tabs.

Activate one lens

In ST-Fin settings, set a single variable to plot — for example Ceiling angle. Keep it to one at a time.

Open the Copilot

Launch the AI Chart Copilot on the same chart.

Ask in plain English

Describe what you want to find. The Copilot reads the oscillator values directly.

Drop the answer into a slot

It suggests a condition and a number. Enter it in the slot, then backtest.

Example prompt · copy & adapt
For long entry, what is the best threshold setting for Ceil angle on this symbol and chart? Analyze the oscillator, find angle values that preceded upward moves, and suggest a condition with a specific number.
Keep prompts in English. The Copilot performs best in English, and ST-Fin's variable names (Ceil angle, Flr dist, PnU area) are already English — so they map cleanly.

02
Path two

CSV export + any AI

Prefer ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini? Export the active lens as CSV and upload it for deeper pattern mining — correlations, multi-bar lookahead, threshold discovery.

Plot one lens

Activate a single variable so the exported column is clean and unambiguous.

Export the data

Use TradingView's export to download the chart data as CSV, including the lens column.

Upload and ask

Drop the CSV into your AI of choice with a precise question about what preceded the moves you care about.

Bring the numbers back

Translate the AI's findings into slot conditions and validate in the Strategy Tester.

Example prompt · copy & adapt
Here is a CSV with the Ceil angle column from a daily BTCUSD chart. For long entry, find the angle values that most often preceded a price increase over the next 3-5 bars. Suggest the best threshold and condition type (above, below, between, or cross).
Another angle · pattern discovery
Using this CSV of Flr distance and Vol buy, tell me whether rising Floor distance combined with high buy volume tends to precede upward moves. Suggest a two-condition rule (A AND B) I can test.
One lens per export keeps the column unambiguous. For multi-variable questions, export each lens and mention both column names in your prompt.
