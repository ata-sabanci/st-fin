Core architecture

How it works, in three steps

📐

1 · Make it dimensionless

Both axes are normalized. Vertical: log(price)/σ using Yang-Zhang volatility. Horizontal: bars/lookback. Angles and areas become comparable across any symbol, timeframe, or volatility regime.

🧊

2 · Freeze the anchors

A lookback window establishes three fixed levels: Ceiling (highest high), Floor (lowest low), and Center (geometric mean). They stay put while price moves — a stable origin to measure from.

📊

3 · Build five triangles

From the frozen anchors to the current bar, five triangles form. Each yields angle, distance, area, and centroid — twenty geometric lenses evolving bar by bar.
